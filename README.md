# Coffee Shop Sales and Demand Analysis

Excel analysis of 149,116 coffee shop transactions covering January to June 2023.

## Project Objective

Analyse revenue growth, store performance, demand patterns, and product mix to identify practical operational opportunities.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Excel Tables and calculated columns
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Slicers
- Data-quality checks
- Business analysis and reporting

## Key Findings

- Revenue increased by 103.8% between January and June.
- Transaction demand peaked at 10:00 and declined by 47.3% at 11:00.
- Coffee and Tea generated 66.74% of total revenue.
- Store performance was relatively balanced, with a 2.8% difference between the highest- and lowest-revenue stores.
